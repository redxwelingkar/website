Prakalam - A pen that writes in air

The Problem

Traditional digital note-taking tools are costly or restrictive, lacking the natural flow of handwriting and true creative freedom.


How It Works

  • Activate the app — your camera starts a live feed.
  • Tap the touch sensor to enable the light source.
  • Move and create — strokes appear in real time.
  • Adjust thickness using a potentiometer, just like a pen.
  • Save notes directly on your device.

The Setup

  • 401 - Mobile
  • 402 - Prakalam Pen
  • 403 - Mobile stand

Key Features

  • Surface-free writing on any surface or in mid-air.
  • Smart stroke detection for precision tracking.
  • Dynamic stroke thickness control.
  • Instant sharing via Email.
  • Lightweight, portable, and eco-friendly.

Ideal For

  • Students taking lecture notes anywhere.
  • Designers sketching creative ideas on the go.
  • Professionals brainstorming in meetings.
  • Accessibility users seeking flexible writing tools.

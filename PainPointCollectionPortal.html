Pain Point Collection Portal

A digital bridge that connects student insights with faculty guidance to transform real-world problems into impactful academic projects.


Overview

The Pain Points Collection Portal is a web-based platform designed to simplify the process of identifying and addressing challenges faced by students during field visits. It enables seamless collaboration between students and faculty to turn observations into meaningful innovations.


Purpose

This platform encourages experiential learning by allowing students to record issues from the field, while faculty members review, validate, and select relevant challenges for project development. The goal is to ensure that every academic effort aligns with real-world industry and community needs.

How It Works

  • Students submit their field observations and challenges via the online portal.
  • Submissions are categorized under predefined domains (e.g., agriculture, healthcare, education, sustainability).
  • Faculty members review and provide constructive feedback.
  • Validated problems are shortlisted for project development or research exploration.

Key Features

  • User-friendly interface for quick submissions and feedback.
  • Structured domains for organized problem collection.
  • Faculty dashboard for efficient review and selection.
  • Real-time tracking of student submissions and approvals.
  • Supports alignment of academic projects with industry challenges.
  • Promotes collaboration, innovation, and practical learning.
